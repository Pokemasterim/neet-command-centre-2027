RELEASE NOTES

What's New in v2.4.2

✨ New in Version 2.4.2
  • Flowmodoro (Count-Up) Study Mode: Switch between Countdown and open-ended Count-Up stopwatch in Study Timer and Focus Mode, with automatic break suggestions when you finish.
  • Active Recall Flashcard Mode: Flip through your saved Chapter Notes and cleaned NCERT Facts as interactive flashcards with Again / Good / Easy practice ratings.
  • Automated Spaced Repetition: Revision Intelligence now automatically picks your next revision date based on your recall confidence (Weak, Medium, or Strong) and revision stage.
  • Cleaner NCERT Facts & Faster Startup: Removed incorrect, duplicate, and broken NCERT fact entries and moved the facts bank into a lightweight external file so the app opens much faster on all phones.
  • Many Bugs Fixed: Fixed display font glitches, corrected the initial 81-chapter counter on Overview, and improved overall polish.
Version 2.4.1
  • Daily Study Hour Leaderboard: Added a Today / This Week toggle on the Global Leaderboard so you can check the Top 20 daily study hours alongside weekly rankings.
  • Clickable "Focusing Now" Live Popup: Tap the Focusing Now pill on the top bar to see which Study Friends and aspirants are actively focusing right now (plus live badges on your Friends list and Today's Top 20 study hours).
  • Music On/Off on Mobile Top Bar: Restored the Ambient Music toggle button on narrow phone screens for quick one-tap access.
  • Older Android Phone Compatibility: Improved compatibility for older Android System WebView versions so all buttons and screens work smoothly without affecting newer phones.
Version 2.4
  • 8,000 NCERT Rapid-Revision Facts: Expanded the daily facts bank from 43 to all 8,000 high-yield NCERT facts across Biology (4,000), Chemistry (2,000), and Physics (2,000).
  • Complete 81-Chapter NEET Syllabus: Added missing chapters — Structural Organisation in Animals, p-Block Elements, and Principles Related to Practical Chemistry — automatically synced for existing users without losing progress.
  • Smarter 4-Pillar Score Predictor: Now combines both your full mock scores and your chapter practice accuracy to estimate your NEET score.
  • Update Changelog Button: Added a Changelog button right inside the update notification banner and Settings so you can always see what changed.
  • Cleaner Mobile Topbar: Reduced clutter on narrow phone screens for a smoother mobile experience.
  • Many Bugs Fixed: Fixed theme border and hover colors, safer revision scheduling buttons, lighter undo memory usage, and overall stability improvements.
