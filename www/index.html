RELEASE NOTES

What's New in v2.6

✨ New in Version 2.6
  • Re-NEET 2026 Rank Predictor: Estimate your expected All India Rank, category rank, percentile, +20 marks jump, and MBBS/BDS chances right inside Mock Analytics using official Re-NEET 2026 result data.
  • Pure Fullscreen Timer Mode: Open a completely distraction-free fullscreen clock showing nothing except your timer and a close button — available both outside and inside Focus Mode.
  • NEET Bio-Clock Trainer & Digital OMR Sheet: Train during the real 2:00–5:00 PM exam window with a 180-minute Exam Simulation (30m/10m/5m bells) and practice bubbling on a timed OMR sheet.
  • Focus Calm Tools: Added a Thought Parking Lot inside Focus Mode to dump distracting thoughts and a 60-second Box Breathing guide to calm exam stress.
  • Smarter Upgrades Across Modules: Sort chapters by highest unfinished weightage, track weekly subject balance in Timer, pin daily recurring habits or roll over yesterday's missions, star tricky flashcards, test "What-If" mock scores, and share a 7-day weekly chart with parents.
  • Free Web App Notifications & 720 Home Screen Logo: Allow free browser notifications on Chrome/Desktop, get stronger background reminders on Android, and see the 720 logo when adding the website to your home screen on iOS, macOS, or Windows.
  • Cleaner NCERT Facts & Refreshed Study Tips: Fixed hundreds of NCERT formulas, arrows, and chapter tags, and updated the rotating Study Tips.
  • Many Bugs Fixed: Fixed Flashcard popup layout, Light Mode styling, late-night custom day reset dates, chapter weightage matching, and improved Lite Mode speed.
Version 2.5
  • Mock Intelligence 2.0: Completely redesigned Mock Analytics with 10 organised tabs — Overview with date filters, Hybrid Quick + Detailed Entry, 4 Subject Dashboards, Practice-Grounded Chapter Heatmap (with editable/deletable question logs), Mistake & Time Analysis, 9 Progression Graphs, Side-by-Side Comparison, Statistical Score Predictor, Automatic Improvement Planner, and Individual Reports with CSV/JSON Backup & Undo/Redo.
  • NEET Chapter Weightage Badges: Added chapter-wise NEET weightage and average question count right beside each chapter in Chapter Tracker.
  • Final Countdown Tick Sound: Added an optional Doomsday Clock tick-tock sound on the Overview Final Countdown with a one-tap On/Off toggle.
  • Reorder Daily Missions: Added up/down buttons so you can rearrange the sequence of your added missions anytime.
  • Smoother Ambient Study Music: Rebuilt the offline soundscapes with seamless audio looping to eliminate stutters and glitches.
  • Smarter Update Checks & Status Indicator: Checks for new releases more frequently, fixes the Online/Offline indicator in Guest Mode, fixes today's study minutes in Focusing Now, and fixes the Changelog popup buttons.
  • Quick First-Time Setup & Custom Day Reset Time: New 2-step setup wizard on first sign-in to set your Doctor Name, NEET Target, Exam Date, Weakest Subject, Night Reminder, and Custom Day Reset Time (12 AM – 5 AM) so late-night study still counts toward today.
  • Past Daily Reflections ("Close My Day" Log): View all your saved daily ratings, mistake tags, and notes in Study History, inside the Close My Day modal, and see your latest reflection right on Overview.
  • Many Bugs Fixed: Improved overall reliability, mobile responsiveness, and data safety across modules.
Version 2.4.2
  • Flowmodoro (Count-Up) Study Mode: Switch between Countdown and open-ended Count-Up stopwatch in Study Timer and Focus Mode, with automatic break suggestions when you finish.
  • Active Recall Flashcard Mode: Flip through your saved Chapter Notes and cleaned NCERT Facts as interactive flashcards with Again / Good / Easy practice ratings.
  • Automated Spaced Repetition: Revision Intelligence automatically picks your next revision date based on your recall confidence (Weak, Medium, or Strong) and revision stage.
  • Cleaner NCERT Facts & Faster Startup: Removed incorrect, duplicate, and broken NCERT fact entries and moved the facts bank into a lightweight external file.
  • Many Bugs Fixed: Fixed display font glitches and corrected the 81-chapter counter on Overview.
Version 2.4.1
  • Daily Study Hour Leaderboard: Added a Today / This Week toggle on the Global Leaderboard.
  • Clickable "Focusing Now" Live Popup: See which Study Friends and aspirants are actively focusing right now.
  • Music On/Off on Mobile Top Bar: Restored the Ambient Music toggle button on narrow phone screens.
  • Older Android Phone Compatibility: Improved compatibility for older Android System WebView versions.
